04 / About Snapshot

A Working Developer, Not a Template

I study Business Information Technology at JKUAT while engineering real software systems. My academic training gives me a double lens: I understand business operations, supply chains, and accounting workflows just as deeply as database normalization, query execution, and API endpoints.

In local Kenyan businesses, software failures rarely happen because the UI wasn't shiny enough. They happen because inventory math didn't account for concurrent orders, staff roles weren't restricted, or offline mobile sync corrupted data. That is why I engineer systems from the database outward.
